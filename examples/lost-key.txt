// A longer story in its own file, loaded with @[Pixi.load](examples/lost-key.txt)
story lost-key "The Lost Key"
stage 16:9 #1d2433
option textspeed=50

bg   home    base:room
bg   street  base:village
bg   park    base:forest
bg   evening base:night

char mia   "Mia"     base:girl    color=#e8556d voice="en-US Female"
char opa   "Grandpa" base:grandpa color=#6f8b4c voice="en-GB Male" pitch=0.8
char robo  "Robo"    base:robot   color=#4fa3d1 voice="en-US Male" pitch=0.6

var clues = 0
var helped = false

# start
title "The Lost Key" "a story in three scenes"
bg home fade 1
show opa thinking at right fade 0.6
opa: Mia, have you seen my garden key? I cannot find it anywhere.
show mia surprised at left from offleft
mia: Your key? Let me help you, Grandpa!
? Search the house first   -> house
? Ask the neighbours       -> street

# house
mia thinking: Where would a key hide in this room?
hotspot 0.65,0.40,0.24,0.30 -> desk   "Desk"
hotspot 0.10,0.15,0.25,0.38 -> window "Window"
? Go outside -> street

# desk
sound base:knock
mia: Just pencils and old letters … but here is a note: "the robot knows".
set clues += 1
-> street

# window
mia: Through the window I can see Robo in the park!
set clues += 1
-> street

# street
bg street fade 1
hide opa
show mia at center
move mia to left 1s
mia: Outside! Clues so far: {clues}.
show robo happy at right from offright
robo: Beep! Hello Mia. I found something shiny in the park.
? Follow Robo into the park       -> park  [helped = true]
? Say no and go home              -> giveup

# park
bg park fade 1
show robo at right
mia happy: Show me!
robo: It was right here, under the big tree …
shake small
sound base:magic
flash #ffffcc 0.5s
robo laugh: Found it!
if clues >= 1: mia: Just like the note said!
-> finale

# finale
bg evening fade 1.5
tint evening 1
hide robo
show opa happy at right fade 0.5
show mia happy at left
opa laugh: My key! Thank you, Mia{helped ? " and Robo" : ""}!
sound base:success
end found "Grandpa can open his garden again."

# giveup
bg home fade 1
show opa sad at right
opa: Never mind, Mia. Maybe tomorrow.
end lost "The key stays lost … for now."
